Step 2: Express the volume using the cylindrical shell method.<br/>
When rotating a region bounded by `y = f(x)`, the `x`-axis, and vertical lines `x = a` and `x = b` about the `y`-axis, the volume `V` is given by the cylindrical shell formula:<br/>
`V = int_a^b (2 pi x f(x)) d x`<br/>
Each shell at horizontal position `x` has circumference `2 pi x` and height `f(x)`, and `d x` is the shell's thickness. Multiplying these gives the volume of a thin shell, and integrating from `x = a` to `x = b` sums all shells to get the total volume.<br/>
