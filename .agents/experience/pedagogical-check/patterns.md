# Cross-Case Patterns: pedagogical-check

_AI-maintained. Promoted from repeated pedagogical findings._
_Read by default before `lessons.md`._

---

## Step Clarity vs Accuracy

If the mathematical claim is correct but the student-facing explanation skips the bridge that justifies it, classify the issue under `step_clarity` first.

**Applies to:** REVIEW, CLASSIFY

---

## Scope Evidence First

Future-learning, wording-reject, and method-reject findings require direct book evidence or `check-future-learning`. Do not raise chapter-boundary P1 findings from intuition alone.

**Applies to:** REVIEW, CLASSIFY

---

## Variable-Consistent Notation

When a worked law statement or branch text drifts from the template's active variables, treat it as a notation issue before treating it as an accuracy issue.

**Applies to:** REVIEW
